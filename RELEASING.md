## Suggested release description

**APO Soundboard Scratch Edition v0.9.8** is the high-fidelity alternate edition for Windows x64 / Equalizer APO. It adds interactive forward/reverse waveform scratching, continuously sustained audio when the cursor is held still, and independent live Pitch/Auto Sweep, while retaining the 16-pad soundboard, hotkeys and local monitoring.

### Highlights
- Full-spectrum FFT overlap-add scratch processing with higher vocal/noise detail than the earlier oscillator/granular prototypes.
- Adaptive use of original decoded PCM near natural speed at neutral Pitch.
- Separate **Select Range** and **Scratch** modes; selection shading disappears in Scratch.
- Refreshed Windows-style Soundboard/Diagnostics tabs.
- Fixed diagnostics ghost borders and overlapping text during resizing/tab switching.
- Immediate Light/Dark repaint of waveform information strips and visible controls.
- Centered bold Speed and Depth values; cleaner layout and instructions.
- Matched Scratch-specific DLL and controller, separate from lightweight v0.9.0.

### Requirements
Windows x64; Equalizer APO x64 on the microphone/capture path. Extract the complete archive and load `APOSoundboardScratch_v0.9.8.dll` in Equalizer APO; keep `APOSoundboardScratchController.exe` alongside it. Close older Scratch controllers first.

### Notes
Scratch DSP uses synthesis during nonstandard speed and freeze, so pitch-preserved material can still acquire artifacts on transients. Native Ogg/Opus decoding is not included. This is the user-accepted Scratch UI build; other Windows systems and setups should still be tested. Stable v0.9.0 remains unchanged.

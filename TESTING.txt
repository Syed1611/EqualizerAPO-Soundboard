Scratch Edition v1.0.0 — Windows acceptance checklist

1. Run the controller, inspect the title bar, taskbar, Alt+Tab and Explorer: the cyan waveform/red vertical line icon should appear at all sizes.
2. Right-click EXE and VST DLL > Properties > Details: FileVersion 1.0.0.0.
3. In Equalizer APO capture/microphone chain, load `APOSoundboardScratch_v1.0.0.dll`, open its matching controller and confirm it connects with no protocol/build mismatch.
4. Play multiple pads; in Scrub, left-click a playing pad at different waveform positions, drag, hold, release to pause, and resume with Play and Space. Other pads must continue unaffected.
5. Repeat 10+ fast drag-release-drag cycles. No persistent buzz/skrr, clicks or jumps; verify Pitch and Auto Sweep independently.
6. Switch Soundboard/Diagnostics, Select Range/Scrub, Light/Dark and resize repeatedly. Look for blank controls, stale text, borders, flashing and misalignment.
7. Verify Space does nothing outside the Scrub waveform mode, while editing labels/values or during hotkey capture.
8. Select local Monitor. Unplug/disconnect the audio output while listening; monitoring must fail cleanly, report device error and not stop the mic VST stream. Reconnect, refresh and manually reenable. This test must be done cautiously, at safe volume.
9. Verify peak and host-output clipped frames are distinct from VST ring underruns and monitor underruns/overruns/errors in Diagnostics.
10. Check config migration from previous Scratch versions, pad reload/remove, missing files, corrupted config backup and graceful shutdown; monitor RAM/handles/GDI counts for leaks.
11. Check 44.1/48/96 kHz hosts, soundpad hotkeys when unfocused, controller close/restart and DPI scaling on a second display.
12. Run Defender or comparable malware scan on the downloaded release archive on Windows.

Offline build checks: all 10 test programs completed; test_scratch skipped an optional external raw voice fixture, documented in README.md. No live host or driver integration was available here.
